01About

Senior visual designer and design lead — art direction, team leadership and hands-on craft for games, iGaming and brands.

I built the visual pipeline from scratch at Gameskraft in India, and I now design the visuals of the Betano platform at Kaizen Gaming Brazil — one of the country's largest betting and casino products. The hero pieces at the top of the site, the promotional banners, the stories: the first thing a player sees.

I pair illustration and brand craft with UX thinking and AI-accelerated workflows, which is how a campaign world goes from brief to live placement in days rather than weeks. I also brief, review and approve the work of a partner agency team, holding quality across everything that ships.
